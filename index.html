Privacy Policy for Focus Freak

Last updated: August 2026

Focus Games ("we," "us," or "our") developed Focus Freak (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and who it may be shared with. By downloading or playing Focus Freak, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

Focus Freak does not require you to create an account, and we do not directly collect your name, email address, or other personal contact information. The App does use third-party services that automatically collect certain technical and usage information, described below.

Google Firebase Analytics

Focus Freak uses Firebase Analytics to understand how players interact with the game, such as which levels are played, how many attempts a level takes, and where players tend to stop playing. This helps us fix bugs and improve level difficulty. Firebase Analytics may collect:

  • App usage events (level starts, attempts, completions, block progress)
  • Device information (device model, operating system version, language, general region)
  • App instance identifiers used to distinguish anonymous, aggregated usage patterns

This information is not used to personally identify you. For more detail on how Google processes this data, see Firebase's privacy and security page.

Google AdMob (Advertising)

Focus Freak displays interstitial ads through Google AdMob to help support free development of the game. AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising identifiers (such as your device's Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and general location (typically city/region level, not precise GPS)
  • Ad interaction data (ads shown, clicked, or dismissed) used for measurement and fraud prevention

This data may be used by Google to serve and measure ads, and may be shared with Google's advertising partners under Google's own privacy practices. You can review or adjust your ad personalization settings at Google Ads Settings, and learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

In-App Purchases

Focus Freak offers an optional "Remove Ads" purchase through Google Play's billing system. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play — we do not receive or store your payment card details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed, which we use to disable ads on your device going forward.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your information. Data collected through Firebase Analytics and AdMob is processed by Google in accordance with Google's own privacy policies, as described above. We do not share any additional data with other third parties beyond what is described in this policy.

Children's Privacy

Focus Freak is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below so we can address it.

Your Choices

  • You can reset or limit your device's advertising identifier in your device's system settings.
  • You can purchase "Remove Ads" within the App to stop future ad displays.
  • You can uninstall the App at any time, which stops all further data collection from your device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if we add new features or services to the App. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date above.
